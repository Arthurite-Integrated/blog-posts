## **Why Quick Suite Was Introduced: The Pain Points Businesses Face**

Before we talk about what Quick Suite does, it is worth understanding why AWS felt compelled to build it. The enterprise productivity landscape had a fundamental problem — not a shortage of data, but a surplus of disconnected tools with no intelligent layer binding them together.

**Data Fragmentation Across Dozens of Systems:** Business users often need to gather data across multiple applications — pulling customer details, checking performance metrics, reviewing internal product information, and performing competitive intelligence. Pulling these together for a single decision could take days.

**Specialist Dependency and Bottlenecks:** Non-technical business users had to rely on data analysts or engineers to extract insights from complex datasets, creating structural bottlenecks and slowing decision cycles across the teams that most needed to act quickly.

**Manual, Repetitive Work at Scale:** Processes such as responding to RFPs, preparing customer briefings, processing invoices, and merchant onboarding consumed enormous amounts of human effort. Quick Suite helps users automate tasks — from routine, daily tasks like responding to RFPs and preparing for customer meetings to automating the most complex business processes such as invoice processing.

**AI That Could Not Bridge Insight and Action:** Most enterprise AI tools were good at answering questions but stopped short of acting on those answers. The gap between insight and action remained wide, manual, and frustrating — undermining the ROI of AI investment.

**Security and Governance Gaps:** Many AI productivity tools embedded within third-party ecosystems did not give enterprise IT departments the data visibility and compliance flexibility they needed. For regulated industries especially, this was a meaningful barrier to adoption.

Amazon Quick Suite was designed from the ground up to address all five of these challenges in a single, cohesive platform.

**The Capabilities of Amazon Quick Suite**

Quick Suite combines agentic teammates for research, business intelligence, and automation into a unified digital workspace for day-to-day work. Here is what each component does.

**Quick Index — The Unified Knowledge Foundation.**

Quick Index operates in the background to bring together all your data — from databases and data warehouses to documents and email — creating a single, intelligent knowledge base that makes AI responses more accurate and reduces time spent searching for information. It connects to Amazon S3, Snowflake, Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint, Exchange, and more.

**Quick Research — Expert-Level Insights on Demand.**

Quick Research is a powerful agent that conducts comprehensive research across your enterprise data and external sources to deliver contextualised, actionable insights in minutes or hours — work that previously could take longer. It builds structured research plans, validates findings against original sources, and delivers cited conclusions your teams can present with confidence.

**Quick Sight — Business Intelligence for Everyone.**

Quick Sight provides AI-powered business intelligence capabilities that transform data into actionable insights through natural language queries and interactive visualisations, helping everyone make faster decisions and achieve better business outcomes. No SQL, no waiting for an analyst. You can also respond to insights with one-click actions — creating tickets, sending alerts, or triggering workflows directly from your dashboards.

**Quick Flows — No-Code Automation for Business Users.**

With Quick Flows, any user can automate repetitive tasks by describing their workflow using natural language without requiring any technical knowledge. Quick Flows fetches information from internal and external sources, takes action in business applications, generates content, and handles process-specific requirements.

**Quick Automate — Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent Automation.**

For complex, cross-departmental processes, Quick Automate deploys multiple specialised AI agents that collaborate to complete tasks end to end — from merchant onboarding to compliance workflows.

**Embedded Chat — AI Where Your People Already Work.**

Embedded Chat enables you to embed Quick Suite's conversational AI directly into your own applications — your CRM, support console, or analytics portal — eliminating the need to build conversational interfaces, orchestration logic, or data access layers from scratch.

**Security and Pricing.**

Quick Suite runs entirely within a company's AWS environment, meaning enterprise data never leaves the organisation's secure cloud boundary. Customer data is never used to train foundation models. And at $20 per user per month, Quick Suite sits meaningfully below Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini Enterprise, both priced around $30 per user per month.
