## About the Pilot: 10 Merchants. One Common Problem.

Genie's pilot programme brought together a cohort of 10 small and mid-sized e-commerce businesses operating in Nigeria and surrounding African markets. These merchants sell primarily through WhatsApp, Telegram, and web-based storefronts — the digital channels their customers actually use. Collectively, they process thousands of customer interactions daily, and together they represent the entrepreneurial backbone of Africa's fast-growing digital commerce sector.

Before Genie, every one of these merchants faced the same structural constraint: their ability to grow was capped by their ability to staff. Every customer enquiry needed a human response. Every product question, every order status update, every payment query — answered manually, one at a time, during working hours only.

This model had a ceiling, and these merchants had hit it.

## The Challenges: The Hidden Cost of Manual Commerce

The challenges facing these merchants were not unique — they are structural features of African SMB e-commerce that affect tens of thousands of businesses across the continent.

- **Static websites and manual chat** — human agents required for every product inquiry, order update, and payment question, with no automation at any stage of the customer journey.
- **High labour costs** — customer service staffing consuming a disproportionate share of operating budgets relative to revenue for businesses operating at SMB scale.
- **Slow response times** — delayed replies directly causing abandoned carts and lost sales to competitors who responded faster.
- **Inconsistent experience** — customer interactions varying in quality depending on which staff member responded and when.
- **Limited hours** — customers browsing and buying at all hours while merchants could only respond during business hours, losing transactions to the clock.
- **Capital barriers to AI** — enterprise-grade conversational AI tools priced well beyond what SMB budgets could accommodate, keeping the technology inaccessible.

The competitive disadvantage was structural. Larger platforms had sophisticated automation. These merchants had WhatsApp and determination.

## The Solution: Genie — Enterprise AI, SMB Price Point

Arthurite Integrated designed Genie as a multi-channel AI commerce assistant purpose-built for the African SMB market. It integrates natively with WhatsApp and Telegram — the platforms where Africa's consumers already shop — and operates as a fully autonomous customer engagement layer that handles the entire pre-sale and order management workflow without human intervention.

### Instant Product Discovery

Customers can browse, search, and enquire about products through natural conversation on WhatsApp or Telegram. Genie understands context, maintains the conversation thread, and provides accurate product information, availability, and pricing in real time — matching the responsiveness of a highly attentive human agent, available at any hour.

### Autonomous Order Management

From enquiry to confirmed order, Genie handles the complete customer journey — capturing order details, confirming quantities, processing payment initiation, and providing real-time order status updates. Merchants receive structured order notifications without needing to manage individual conversations.

### Multi-Channel Consistency

Whether a customer reaches through WhatsApp, Telegram, or the merchant's web storefront, they receive the same experience — the same product knowledge, the same response quality, the same speed. Genie eliminates the channel inconsistency that had previously made multi-platform selling administratively overwhelming for small teams.
