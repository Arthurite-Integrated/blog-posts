It’s Friday evening.

You’ve survived another week, closed your laptop, and promised yourself you’re not spending another weekend scrolling through Instagram, watching other people have fun.

There’s just one problem.

You have absolutely no idea what to do.

When people think about going out in Lagos, the first things that usually come to mind are clubs, restaurants, concerts, or maybe the beach. But Lagos has a much wider range of experiences happening every week.

Sometimes, finding something interesting simply means looking beyond your usual weekend routine.

So, if you're wondering what to do in Lagos this weekend, here are seven types of events worth trying.

![](../../../../images/image-9eb7b8e0.jpeg)

## 1. Live Music and Concerts

Lagos and live music naturally belong together.

And you don't necessarily need to wait for a major Afrobeats artist to announce a huge concert before going out.

There are smaller live-band performances, acoustic nights, open-mic sessions, album listening parties and intimate shows that can give you a completely different experience from a stadium-sized concert.

If music is your thing, try exploring beyond the artists you already know. You might discover someone new while having a genuinely good night out.

## 2. Comedy Shows

Sometimes you just want to sit somewhere and laugh for two hours.

Comedy shows are perfect for that.

From established comedians to smaller open-mic nights featuring upcoming talent, Lagos has a thriving comedy scene that can make for an easy weekend plan.

They're also great if you're looking for something that doesn't require much preparation. Get your ticket, show up, grab something to eat or drink, and enjoy the show.

## 3. Art Exhibitions and Cultural Experiences

Not every weekend has to be loud.

Lagos has a strong creative scene spanning photography, visual art, fashion, film and African culture.

An exhibition or cultural event can be a completely different way to spend your Saturday afternoon, especially if your normal weekend consists of restaurants and cinemas.

The best part? You don't have to be an art expert. Walk around. Look at things. Ask questions. Take pictures where permitted. Talk to the artists.

You might be surprised by how much you enjoy it.

## 4. Food Events and Pop-Ups

Going to a restaurant is one thing. Going somewhere specifically to experience food is another.

Food festivals, tasting events, chef pop-ups and themed dining experiences give you an opportunity to try dishes from different vendors without committing your entire evening to one restaurant.

And Lagos being Lagos, there's usually more than food involved. Music, games, vendors and other activities often turn these events into full social experiences.

Come hungry. Obviously.

## 5. Tech and Professional Meetups

Your weekend can be fun and still accidentally help your career.

Developer meetups, startup gatherings, business conferences, design communities, product events and workshops happen regularly around Lagos.

You might go because you're curious about a topic and leave having met someone working on something interesting, discovered a job opportunity, found a potential collaborator or simply learned something useful.

Unlike formal networking situations, conversations at smaller community events often happen naturally. Sometimes showing up is enough.

## 6. Workshops and Creative Classes

Instead of only consuming experiences, try creating something.

Photography workshops. Painting sessions. Cooking classes. Dance classes. Pottery. Music sessions. Fitness communities. Coding workshops.

These events are particularly good when you want to meet people because everyone already has something in common: you're all there to learn the same thing.

That removes some of the awkwardness of walking into a random social event and wondering how you're supposed to start talking to strangers.

Worst case? You learn something new. Best case? You discover a hobby you didn't know you'd enjoy.

## 7. Social and Community Events

Sometimes you don't need a huge concert or elaborate experience. You just need good people and something interesting happening.

Game nights, book clubs, networking mixers, community hangouts, themed parties, charity events and small gatherings can be some of the most enjoyable events in Lagos.

They're often more relaxed, easier to meet people at and significantly less stressful than some of the city's larger events. And if you've been trying to expand your social circle, these are worth paying attention to.

## The Hard Part Is Usually Finding What's Happening

Lagos isn't short of things to do. The problem is that information about them can be scattered everywhere.

One event is announced on Instagram. Another is buried in someone's WhatsApp status. You hear about another from a friend three days after it happened.

That's why having places where events can actually be discovered and registered for matters.

Platforms like TavernCentral give organizers a place to publish and manage their events while making it easier for people looking for experiences to find something worth attending.

As more organizers put their events online, discovering what to do shouldn't require knowing the right person or following fifty different Instagram pages.

## **Try Something Different This Weekend**

You don't have to attend every event happening in Lagos. You don't even have to become the person who's outside every weekend.

Just occasionally try something outside your normal routine.

Go to the comedy show. Attend the small tech meetup. Try the painting class. Walk into the exhibition. Listen to an artist you've never heard before.

And yes, even if none of your friends are interested, you can still go.
