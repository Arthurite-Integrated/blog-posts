# **The "Over-provisioning" Fallacy**

If you are running on legacy servers, you only have two options to prepare for Q4 traffic spikes, and they both suck.

1. **Under-provision and Crash:** You guess your peak traffic, you guess wrong, the API rate limits max out, the database locks up, and your site goes dark exactly when user intent is highest.
2. **Over-provision and Waste:** You panic and provision massive EC2 instances just in case. You survive the spike, but you end up paying premium cloud prices for compute capacity that sits completely idle 95% of the time.

Legacy auto-scaling is fundamentally reactive. By the time a monolithic server recognizes it needs to spin up more capacity to handle a traffic surge, the bottleneck has already occurred. Your users are staring at a buffering screen, and your developers are scrambling to manually restart choked services.
