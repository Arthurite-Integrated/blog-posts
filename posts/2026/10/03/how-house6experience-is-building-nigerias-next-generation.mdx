**By: Dauda Lawal – Web Developer**

In fine dining, creating an exceptional guest experience is equal parts art and engineering. While immaculate ambiance and memorable flavors win over guests, operational efficiency behind the scenes determines whether a venue succeeds or struggles under its own weight.

For **House6Experience**, a premier dining and hospitality venue in Lagos, Nigeria, success brought a high-volume operational challenge. Serving over 5,400 monthly covers across 45 tables (120 seats), the flagship venue generates nearly ₦100 million in monthly revenue. Yet, like many established fine-dining destinations, its day-to-day operations relied on fragmented, paper-based workflows: phone bookings logged in a diary, orders hand-written by servers, paper dockets pinned to kitchen boards, and manual weekly inventory counts.

The result?

**A 25% reservation no-show rate, order-to-kitchen delays averaging 8 minutes, a 15% order error rate, and over ₦82 million annually lost to operational inefficiencies and avoidable food waste.**

To solve these challenges and scale its brand, House6Experience partnered with [Arthurite Integrated](https://arthuriteintegrated.com/) to architect and deploy a modern, cloud-native Restaurant Management and Connected Restaurant Platform on AWS.

## **Re-Engineering the Restaurant Lifecycle**

Rather than layering disconnected third-party software on top of old habits, Arthurite designed a unified platform covering every touchpoint in the venue's ecosystem. Deployed in the AWS Africa (Cape Town) region and accelerated via Amazon CloudFront, the architecture relies on real-time WebSockets and serverless microservices to synchronize front-of-house, kitchen, and management operations instantaneously.

![](https://arthuriteintegrated.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/image-3-1024x641.png)

### **Core Architecture Highlights**

- **Digital Reservation Engine:** Guests book online 24/7, while automated SMS/email workflows deliver reminders and one-tap reconfirmations. This workflow targets cutting no-shows from **25% to below 8%**, saving an estimated 90 lost covers—and ₦1.7 million—every month.
- **Real-Time Interactive Floor Plan:** Hostesses and managers track every table through eight dynamic statuses (from *Available* to *Bill Requested* and *Cleaning*). The system predicts table turn times, shaving turnover gaps down from 15 minutes to under 8 minutes and boosting peak-period seating capacity by **18%**.
- **Sub-15-Second Kitchen Display System (KDS):** Paper tickets were replaced with 5 station-based digital KDS screens (Hot Kitchen, Cold Kitchen, Pastry, Bar, and Expo). Server orders transmit instantly, reducing order-to-kitchen submission times from **8 minutes to under 15 seconds** and eliminating illegible docket errors.
- **Automated Recipe Costing & Inventory:** Linked to all 85 menu items, the system tracks 320 distinct ingredients in real time. Every time a dish is ordered, ingredients auto-deduct from stock. Automated low-stock alerts and daily theoretical vs. actual variance reports target cutting food costs from **38% down to 30%**, saving **₦2.7 million monthly** in food waste.
- **Unified Nigerian Payment Ecosystem:** Integrating Paystack and Flutterwave, servers process card, transfer, USSD, QR pay, and cash directly from tablets. Split-check handling and automated reconciliation reduce daily finance closing times from **2 hours to 15 minutes**.
- **Connected Restaurant Sensors (AWS IoT Core):** In Phase 2, wireless IoT sensors track cold-storage temperatures (walk-in fridges/freezers), monitor heavy equipment health, and count footfall at the entrance, ensuring food safety and operational uptime.
