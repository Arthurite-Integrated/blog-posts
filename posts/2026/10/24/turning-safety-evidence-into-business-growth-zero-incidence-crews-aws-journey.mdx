For Zero Incidence Crew, fragmented records were affecting more than administration. Enterprise customers needed evidence of robust safety processes, but incident information was scattered across spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected tools.

Around $500,000 in potential contracts was stalled, while audit preparation required almost four weeks of manual effort.

Arthurite Integrated helped the firm establish an AWS platform that brought incident records, supporting evidence, and operational oversight into a more structured environment.

## Architecture Designed for Accountability

![](https://arthuriteintegrated.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/image-8-1024x654.png)

*The supplied reference architecture shows a multi-Availability-Zone application environment, security controls, audit logging, and cross-region backup.*

In this reference design, an Application Load Balancer distributes requests to EC2 application servers in private subnets across two Availability Zones. Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL provides Multi-AZ database resilience.

Amazon S3 stores supporting files with encryption and versioning. The diagram also identifies a disaster-recovery replica in the Ireland Region.

AWS Identity and Access Management supports permissions and multifactor authentication, while AWS KMS supports encryption-key management. Amazon GuardDuty supplies threat findings to AWS Security Hub.

Amazon CloudWatch collects operational metrics and alarms. AWS CloudTrail and VPC Flow Logs provide additional records for reviewing activity and network traffic.

Together, these components support a platform where operational evidence can be collected and reviewed systematically.

## Results That Reached the Sales Pipeline

The published case study reports:

- Approximately **$485,000 in previously stalled contracts closed** within four months.
- Audit preparation reduced to **less than two hours**.
- A recorded compliance score increased from **45% to 98%**.
- Customer satisfaction rose from **71% to 94%**.

The commercial benefit was clear: the team could respond to customer scrutiny with accessible evidence and more consistent processes.
